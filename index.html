NASFIA (2024)

NASFIA is a research and innovation competition among students across Indonesia in the field of science. This activity is intended for Indonesian youth attending junior high school, high school, or equivalent, aged 11 to 18. NASFIA's activities are based on the premise that technological advances have changed the way we approach and view science. Therefore, a platform is needed to explore the scientific potential of Indonesia's young people to advance and compete globally.

As a form of concern for the development of research and innovation in Indonesia, the Indonesian Scientific Society (ISS) is holding the National Science Fair for Indonesian Adolescents (NASFIA) 2024.

Front-end Software Developer - SWIFT (Speedy Way of Informing For Track)

SWIFT (Speedy Way of Informing For Track) is an application for traffic reporting including road conditions, obstacles, road obstructions, damaged road facilities, and even for receiving emergency reports from the public to relevant institutions.

SWIFT prioritizes the aspirations, reports, and speed of action from and for the community to create a sense of security and comfort in residential areas. SWIFT works by receiving mobile-based reports from the public, which will be followed up immediately by the relevant agencies.
